Overview

This course builds up the theory and machinery needed to understand Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) from first principles. Starting from Markov Decision Processes, we develop value functions, the Bellman equation, and dynamic programming, then transition into deep learning foundations and modern deep reinforcement learning methods — value-based, policy-gradient, and actor-critic approaches — culminating in a full derivation of SAC's core equations.
